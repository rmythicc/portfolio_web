ABOUT ME

Who am I?

My name is David, I’m 17 and I’m from the Czech Republic.

I spend most of my time around games, content creation and projects I find interesting. This website is part of that — a place where I share what I do and what I’m working on.

Hobbies

Right now, I mainly focus on games like War Thunder, Wuthering Waves and modded Minecraft.

War Thunder is where I spend most of my time — I enjoy the military aspect, learning mechanics and improving my gameplay.

I won’t lie, I can easily spend more than half of my free day just playing games… but at the same time, it’s what led me to everything else I do.
